# Shopping on the site — how it works for customers

Written so you can hand it to a customer, or use it to answer questions.

---

## You never need an account

You can browse, add to your cart and complete an order without signing up.
There is no "register to continue" wall anywhere on the site.

An account is optional and only does three things: remembers your past orders,
saves your delivery addresses, and keeps a wishlist.

---

## Finding things

- **Search** at the top of every page. It looks at product names, descriptions,
  SKUs and keywords — including Swahili terms, so *ndoo* finds buckets and
  *sufuria* finds cooking pots. Suggestions appear as you type.
- **Categories** groups everything by department.
- **Filters** on the shop and category pages narrow by price, availability, sale
  items and new arrivals. Sorting includes price low-to-high, newest and most
  popular.

Filters are stored in the web address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked or
sent to someone.

---

## The cart

Your cart is saved in your own browser, so you can close the tab and come back
to it. Change quantities with the + and − buttons; you cannot order more than is
in stock.

If something sells out or its price changes while it is sitting in your cart,
you are told when you next open the site — the item is not silently removed or
quietly re-priced.

---

## Checking out

1. **Your details** — name and phone number. Email is optional.
2. **Pickup or delivery.**
   - *Pickup* is free. You are told when your order is packed and ready.
   - *Delivery* asks for county, town and area, then optionally estate,
     building, landmark and directions. Landmarks matter more than street names
     here, so "Opposite the supermarket, blue gate" is genuinely useful.
3. **Payment** — choose M-Pesa, cash on delivery, or pay at pickup, depending on
   what the shop offers.
4. **Place order.**

You then get an order number and a confirmation page.

**Nothing is charged on the website.** No card details are ever asked for. The
shop contacts you to confirm the order and the delivery fee, and you pay after
that.

---

## Ordering on WhatsApp instead

Every WhatsApp button turns whatever you are looking at into a ready-made
message:

- On a product page — that product, its code and price
- In the cart — every item, quantities, and the total
- At checkout — the whole order including your delivery details

Nothing is sent automatically. WhatsApp opens with the message typed out, and
you decide whether to send it.

---

## Tracking your order

Your confirmation page has a status timeline:

**Order placed → Confirmed → Being packed → Out for delivery → Delivered**

Pickup orders show "Ready for pickup" instead of "Out for delivery".

The link works without an account, from the same browser you ordered in. If you
have an account, every order is listed under **My orders** permanently.

---

## Having an account

**Signing up** needs your name, phone number and a password. Email is optional.
Your phone number is how you sign in — it is what you are most likely to
remember.

**If you were already shopping as a guest** and then sign in, your cart is
merged with anything saved to your account. If you had 2 buckets on your phone
and 3 on your laptop, you get 3, not 5.

**Your account gives you:**

- **My orders** — every order with its full detail and current status
- **Saved addresses** — save "Home" and "Work" so repeat orders take seconds.
  The first one you save becomes your default
- **Wishlist** — tap the heart on any product. It stays with your account, so it
  is there on your other devices
- **Profile** — change your name, phone or email, and your password

**Forgot your password?** Enter your phone or email on the reset page. For your
security the site gives the same answer whether or not an account exists, so it
cannot be used to work out who is registered.

> Note for the shop owner: password reset emails and SMS are **not connected
> yet**. Reset links are written to the server log for now, so a customer who is
> locked out needs to contact you directly.

---

## Your privacy

The shop holds your name, phone, optional email, delivery addresses and order
history — what it needs to deliver to you. It is not sold to anyone.

Nobody can read your password, including the shop. Your orders can only be
opened by you.

Marketing messages are **opt-in**. If you did not tick the box, you get order
messages only.

---

## Installing the app

Optional. The website does everything the app does — installing just puts an
icon on your home screen and makes it open faster.

- **Android (Chrome):** a small card offers to install after you have browsed
  for a while. Or use the browser menu → *Install app*.
- **iPhone/iPad (Safari):** tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
- **Computer (Chrome/Edge):** an install icon appears in the address bar.

If you tap **Maybe later**, you will not be asked again for a month.

You are never forced to install anything, never asked to allow notifications,
and never blocked from using the normal website.
